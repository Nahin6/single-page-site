04 — The Ritual

Three slow steps to softer skin

Step 01

Pump

Dispense 1–2 pumps onto dry fingertips. Warm gently between your hands.

Step 02

Massage

Sweep over face and neck in slow circles for 60 seconds to lift away the day.

Step 03

Rinse

Rinse with lukewarm water, pat dry and follow with your favourite serum.

08 — FAQ

Questions, gently answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Write to us at hello@frenava.com.

Is Frenava suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes. The formula is pH-balanced at 5.5, fragrance comes only from natural botanical extracts, and it has been dermatologist tested on sensitive skin.

Does it remove makeup and sunscreen?

Absolutely. Camellia oil dissolves makeup, mascara and SPF. For heavy makeup, massage for a little longer before rinsing.

How long does one bottle last?

Used twice daily with 1–2 pumps, a 150 ml bottle lasts roughly three months.

Is the packaging recyclable?

The bottle is made from recyclable PP and the carton is FSC-certified board printed with soy inks. We also offer a refill pouch with 70% less plastic.

What if it doesn't suit my skin?

Every order is covered by our 30-day happy-skin guarantee. If you're not in love, we'll refund you — no questions asked.
